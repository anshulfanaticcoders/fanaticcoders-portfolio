# FanaticCoders Product Strategy

Use this skill before planning product direction, public pages, service taxonomy, team content, lead capture, or admin-managed portfolio content.

## Defaults
- Present FanaticCoders as a premium web service team, not a generic agency template.
- Center services around design, development, SEO, frontend engineering, backend engineering, WordPress, Laravel, Supabase, and custom themes.
- Make the site useful for clients who need trust signals quickly: services, outcomes, team expertise, proof, process, and contact path.
- Keep admin scope practical: create, edit, sort, publish, and archive content that appears on the public site.

## Public Content Model
- Services: title, slug, summary, long copy, stack tags, deliverables, featured flag, sort order, SEO metadata.
- Team members: name, role, photo, expertise tags, bio, social links, featured flag, sort order.
- Projects: title, slug, client or category, problem, solution, stack, screenshots, results, SEO metadata.
- Testimonials: client name, company, quote, rating or highlight, related service/project.
- Leads: name, email, company, budget range, service interest, message, source page.

## Planning Rules
- Tie every page section to a client decision: trust, fit, capability, quality, or contact.
- Prefer fewer stronger sections over many weak decorative sections.
- Include a clear admin owner for every public content type.
- Defer CRM, invoicing, chat, and project management unless the user explicitly asks.

## Output
- State recommended pages and admin objects.
- Call out what is in scope for the next build step and what is intentionally deferred.
